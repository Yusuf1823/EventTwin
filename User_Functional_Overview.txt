1. EVENTTWIN KYA HAI? (OVERVIEW)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Jab kisi city ya venue (jaise Jio World Convention Centre, BKC, Mumbai) mein 
lakho log kisi mega-event, concert, ya summit ke liye ikkatthe hote hain, toh 
unka crowd management bohot mushkil ho jata hai:
  - Entrance gates par lambi lines aur stampede risk.
  - Metro aur railway stations par crush crowd.
  - Main access roads par heavy traffic gridlock.
  - Ek jagah (Zone A) over-full hoti hai, jabki dusri pass wali jagah (Zone C) 
    khali padi rehti hai.

EventTwin ek A.I.-powered smart operational digital twin platform hai jo city 
operators, event managers, aur traffic authorities ko real-time status dikhata hai, 
aane wali pareshaniyo ko 15 se 45 minute pehle predict karta hai, aur crowd ko 
smartly balance karne ke actionable solutions deta hai.==


2. OPERATOR / USER PERSPECTIVE SE MAIN FEATURES & SCREENS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

[1] COMMAND CENTER (LIVE DASHBOARD)
    - City Health Check: Pure event ecosystem ka overall Stress Level (0-100%) 
      aur live health indicators ek glance mein dikhate hain.
    - 6 Core Operational Telemetry Cards: Live Visitors count, Venue Pressure, 
      Transit Platform Load, Parking Occupancy, Hotel Guest Pressure, aur Road 
      Mobility status.
    - Next Critical Event Forecast Banner: Agar city mein koi bhi resource (gate, 
      transit, ya parking) overcrowd hone wala hai, toh sabse upar warning 
      banner aa jata hai jo batata hai ki kitne minute mein risk aane wala hai.
    - 1-Click Action Shortcut: Directly alert se solution page par navigate 
      karne ka option.


[2] GEOSPATIAL DIGITAL TWIN (REAL MAP VIEW)
    - Real Geography Map: Mumbai ke BKC aur surrounding areas ka interactive map.
    - Live Facility Pins: 15 real locations (Venues, Hotels, Metro/Railway 
      Stations, Parking Lots, aur Shuttle Corridors) map par pins ki tarah 
      render hote hain.
    - View Mode Switcher (Current vs Predicted +30m):
        * "Current Mode": Abhi is waqt kahan kitna crowd hai dikhata hai.
        * "Predicted (+30m Risk) Mode": Visual switch karne par map pins 
          apna color change kar leti hain (Green = Safe, Yellow = Moderate, 
          Orange = High, Red = Critical Risk) taki 30 minute baad hone wale 
          traffic jam aur overcrowding ka pehle se andaza lag jaye.


[3] PREDICTIVE FORECASTING ENGINE (PREDICTIONS PAGE)
    - Multi-Horizon Capacity Matrix: 7 main resources (Zone A, Zone B, Zone C, 
      Venue, Transit, Parking, Hotel) ka agle 15m, 30m, aur 45m ka expected 
      load comparison table.
    - Risk Color Coding: Risk level ke hisab se automatic color badges:
        * LOW (<70%): Normal flow
        * MODERATE (70-85%): Attention required
        * HIGH (85-100%): Severe pressure
        * CRITICAL (>100%): Saturation breakeven
    - Causal Ripple Explanations (Kyun Congestion Ho Raha Hai): Pure plain 
      language mein batata hai ki bottleneck ka asli karan kya hai (e.g., 
      "Baarish ki wajah se shuttles slow hui hain aur +20% visitor inflow badha hai").
    - Embedded What-If Controls: Page par hi live parameters change karke check 
      kar sakte hain ki naye inputs se future predictions par kya asar padega.
    - Preempt Bottleneck Trigger: Instant button daba kar crowd ko overloaded 
      Zone A se khali Zone C (Kalina Hub) ki taraf divert/rebalance karne ka 
      simulation chala sakte hain.


[4] WHAT-IF CRISIS SIMULATOR (SIMULATOR PAGE)
    - Custom Crisis Testing Sliders: User alag-alag emergency ya shock 
      scenarios build kar sakta hai:
        * Visitor Increase Slider (0% se +100%)
        * Heavy Rain / Weather Impact (0% se 50%)
        * Transit Disruption / Train Delays (0% se 50%)
        * Parking Capacity Closure (0% se 50%)
        * Venue Check-in Gate Delays (0 se 90 minutes)
    - Quick Preset Buttons: 1-click presets jaise "Heavy Rain + Transit Down" 
      ya "Evening Peak Surge".
    - Before vs After Comparison Table: Dikhata hai ki bina kisi action ke 
      city stress kitna hoga vs Smart Rebalancing apply karne ke baad kitna 
      control ho jayega.
    - A.I. Prescriptive Directives: Automatically recommend karta hai ki traffic 
      ko kahan divert karna chahiye aur kitna stress kam hoga.


[5] OPERATIONS & ACTION DISPATCH (OPERATIONS PAGE)
    - Prescriptive Intervention Action Cards:
        1. REDIRECT VISITORS: Overcrowded Zone A se 15-20% crowd ko Zone C 
           concourses ki taraf divert karna.
        2. REROUTE SHUTTLES: Congested main highway se hatakar shuttles ko 
           bypass mobility corridors par shift karna.
        3. REDISTRIBUTE PARKING: Full venue parking (92% full) se cars ko 
           Kalina overflow staging lot (8,000+ free bays) mein direct karna.
    - 3-Stage Dispatch Lifecycle:
        * SIMULATE ACTION (Effect analyze karo)
        * ACTION APPROVED (Management clearance)
        * READY TO DISPATCH (Ground staff / traffic control message send)


[6] PRIORITY ALERTS FEED (ALERTS PAGE)
    - Triage Categorization:
        * 🔴 PREDICTED CRITICAL RISK: Agle 45 min mein 100% capacity cross karne 
          wale spots.
        * 🔴 CRITICAL: Current active bottlenecks aur gate delays.
        * 🟡 ATTENTION: Operational warnings (e.g. hotel check-in delay, 
          parking 75% full).
        * 🔵 A.I. INSIGHT: Available buffer options (e.g. Zone C mein 8,250 free 
          parking bays available).
    - Clear Root-Cause Structure: Har alert mein 3 baatein clearly likhi hoti hain:
        * Kya Hua (What Happened)
        * Kyun Matter Karta Hai (Why It Matters)
        * Kya Action Lena Chahiye (What Can Be Done)


[7] TEMPORAL ANALYTICS & TRENDS (ANALYTICS PAGE)
    - Historical & Forecasted Stress Curves: Past 12-24 hours ka hourly stress 
      trend dekhne ke sath agle 45 minutes ka forecasted stress trajectory 
      overlay line graph par dikhti hai.
    - Cross-Sector Telemetry: Venue load, Transit load, aur Parking load ka 
      side-by-side comparative graph.
    - Timeframe Filters: 2H, 6H, 12H, aur 24H views.


[8] PLATFORM CLEARANCE & SETTINGS (SETTINGS PAGE)
    - Event Profile Configuration: Active event title (e.g. "Mumbai Global Expo 2026") 
      aur scale customize karna.
    - Custom Alarm Thresholds: Operational stress warning triggers ko 70% se 95% 
      par adjust karne ka control.
    - User Role Clearance: Security clearance view (City Operations, Event 
      Organizer, Transport Authority, Hospitality Lead).


3. EVENTTWIN USE KARNE KE MAIN OPERATIONAL ADVANTAGES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Early Warning System: Congestion hone se 15-45 minute pehle pata chal jata 
   hai, jisse reactive police management ki bajaye proactive mitigation hoti hai.
2. Spatial Rebalancing: Single zone overload hone par city ki under-utilized 
   capacities (Zone C Kalina) ko fully leverage kiya jata hai.
3. Easy Decision Making: Har alert ke sath actionable guidance milti hai, 
   jisse decision-making lightning fast ho jati hai.
4. Smooth Event Experience: Visitors ko gate stampede, severe traffic jam, aur 
   parking hunt se azadi milti hai.

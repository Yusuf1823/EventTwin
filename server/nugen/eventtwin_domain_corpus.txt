1. DOMAIN OPERATIONAL CONTEXT & OBJECTIVE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EventTwin is an operational geospatial digital twin and predictive incident command 
system designed for metropolitan mega-events (500,000+ attendees) centered at the 
Jio World Convention Centre (JWCC) in Bandra Kurla Complex (BKC), Mumbai, Maharashtra, 
India.

The operational objective of the domain-aligned artificial intelligence is:
1. Continuous spatial state estimation across interconnected transit, venue, parking, 
   and hospitality nodes.
2. Predictive bottleneck identification 15 to 45 minutes before safety thresholds are breached.
3. Modeling non-linear cascade ripple effects caused by severe Mumbai monsoon downpours, 
   tidal waterlogging, transit chokepoints, and gate delays.
4. Prescribing strict-conservation spatial rebalancing interventions that relieve critical 
   stress in Zone A (Core Event Precinct) by transferring demand to surplus buffers in 
   Zone C (Kalina Spillover Hub).

3. MONSOON WEATHER IMPACT & HOSPITALITY CASCADING RIPPLE LAWS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mumbai experiences extreme precipitation events governed by Indian Meteorological 
Department (IMD) warning bands:
- Light Drizzle (<2.5 mm/hr): Negligible road disruption; umbrella handling adds 5% gate dwell.
- Moderate Rain (2.5 - 7.5 mm/hr): Road traffic slows by 15-25%; shuttle turnaround expands to 18-20m.
- Heavy Rain (7.5 - 15.0 mm/hr): Road capacity drops by 35-45%; suburban local trains experience delays; 
  shuttle turnaround stretches to 22-26m.
- Cloudburst & Torrential Rain (>15.0 mm/hr):
  * Mithi River Tidal Overflow: Northern BKC drainage boundary experiences tidal backflow.
  * Kurla Western Underpass waterlogging forces closure of vehicular ingress lanes.
  * Workforce Absenteeism: Suburban transit waterlogging leads to 15-35% staff delays across hotels and venue gates.

Hospitality Cascading Ripple Effects:
1. Stranded Delegate Influx: Severe storms strand outdoor attendees; guests immediately seek 
   emergency shelter at Zone A hotels (Trident, Sofitel), surging occupancy by +15% to +25%, 
   often reaching 100% capacity.
2. Peripheral Cancellation: Distant airport and South Mumbai hotels experience 8-15% booking 
   cancellations due to flooded vehicular arterial corridors.
3. Food & Beverage Consumption Shift: Outdoor food kiosks and staging stalls suffer 80-95% demand 
   collapse; indoor restaurants, lounges, and convention concessionaires experience a +35% to +45% 
   demand wave.
4. Logistics & Dewatering Deployment: Municipal emergency dewatering pumps (up to 24 units) deploy 
   at Kurla subway and Kalina swales; emergency rain poncho distribution stock (150,000+ units) 
   mobilizes at Gate 1-4 concourses.
